# Beacon Design System

Beacon is the shared component library used by every customer-facing Helix
application. It ships tokens, accessible primitives and Figma parity.

## Adoption

- 14 product squads migrated in the beta programme
- Dark mode and density tokens are available
- Accessibility audit passed in September 2026
