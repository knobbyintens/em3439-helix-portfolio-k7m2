# Aurora Analytics Platform

Aurora is the internal analytics workspace that lets product teams explore
event streams and build reusable dashboards without leaving the browser.

## Capabilities

- Streaming query engine over the warehouse
- Shared dashboard library
- Scheduled exports to the reporting pipeline

## Ownership

Data Platform Guild (platform-data@helix.example)
